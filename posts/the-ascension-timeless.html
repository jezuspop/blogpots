The Ascension

What did Jesus give his followers when he returned to the Father, and why does that matter to us now?

  • During times of transition, my faith has been tested:
    • Can I focus on Jesus when everything is unstable?
    • Am I looking for external victories to feel faithful?
    • Am I too reliant on myself or on other people's faith?
  • When I get to this place, I go back to the Ascension — it puts things in perspective
  • Questions we sometimes ask:
    • "I would be in a better place if I had Jesus to walk with like the Apostles"
    • "I would grow more if Jesus discipled me instead of my current mentor"
    • "I have outgrown this ministry. Who is qualified to teach me?"
  • What if we could see miracles in person, witness His showdowns with religious leaders, hear His sermons, have direct access to the Savior?

Big Idea

If you've had thoughts like "I would be stronger if I could walk with Jesus in person," you may be missing the significance of the Ascension. Through it, Jesus offers something infinitely more fulfilling than physical presence.

1

The Importance of the Ascension

"Jesus said, 'Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet ascended to the Father. Go instead to my brothers and tell them, I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.'"

John 20:17 (NIV)
  • Mary Magdalene was close to Jesus — cured of 7 demons (Luke 8:2), with His family at the crucifixion
  • She's trying to hold on for dear life, not wanting to lose Him again
  • Strange response from Jesus: "Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet ascended"
  • Why? Jesus was offering something much more fulfilling than a physical connection
  • Mary was thinking: "I don't want to lose you again"
  • Jesus was thinking: "I'm trying to make it impossible for that to ever happen"
  • But it could only come after He ascended
2

The Holy Spirit Through Ascension

"And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another advocate to help you and be with you forever—the Spirit of truth... I will not leave you as orphans; I will come to you."

John 14:16-18 (NIV)
  • Jesus teaches that the relationship will get BETTER because of the Ascension (v. 28 — "You should be glad I am going to the Father")
  • If Jesus did not ascend, the Holy Spirit would not be with us
  • Jesus in physical form was limited:
    • Limited to one place at one time
    • Could be physically taken from us
    • Could die due to His physical nature
  • Imagine trying to see Him today:
    • How long would the waiting list be for a prayer time, coffee, marriage counsel, wedding, Bible study?
    • With 7 billion people on earth, it would be like Moses judging 2 million Israelites morning to night
  • But through the Ascension:
    • Jesus expanded across the universe
    • Every baptized disciple receives the Holy Spirit
    • He is infinitely available to everyone — no lines, no stress, full access
  • Colossians 1:27 — "This is the secret: Christ lives in you"
3

The Power of the Ascension

"I tell you, among those born of women there is no one greater than John; yet the one who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he."

Luke 7:28 (NIV)
  • Jesus calls John the Baptist the GREATEST prophet — greater than Abraham, Moses, Elijah, Isaiah, Daniel
  • If Jesus said that about me, I could just die right there
  • But catch the ending: The LEAST in the kingdom of God is greater than John!
  • Somewhere in the world is the weakest Christian — the most sinful, insecure, faithless, fearful, troubled
  • The person you would never headline a conference
  • That person is greater than John the Baptist and the history of great prophets!
  • Why? Because of the Ascension
    • The Holy Spirit is in every one of us
    • Giving us our own personal Jesus to take with us

"Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ himself?... Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own."

1 Corinthians 6:15, 19 (NIV)

Conclusion

  • Summary: You have a greater chance at a close relationship with Jesus than those who physically walked with Him. We are the hands and feet of Jesus — His witnesses, His ambassadors.
  • Call to Action: We have no excuses — no excuse not to grow deeply, no excuse to say we can never change, no excuse to believe the Apostles had the best God could offer.
  • Closing: View your faith and connection to Jesus as just as real as if He was with you in physical form. Maybe, just maybe, the Ascension will encourage us to live differently tomorrow.
