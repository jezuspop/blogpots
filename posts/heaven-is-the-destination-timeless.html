Heaven Is the Destination

Heaven is the destination. From pre-flight to landing, keep in mind who's waiting to greet you and what you won't have to carry anymore.

  • Think about the last time you flew somewhere. Pre-flight feels like a long list of things to get through, and the flight can be uncomfortable. What makes all of it worth doing?
  • The Christian life is like that flight. The destination makes all the difference.

Big Idea

Heaven is the destination. From pre-flight to landing, keep in mind who's waiting to greet you and what you won't have to carry anymore.

1

Pre-Flight: Before Conversion

Where are you headed?

  • Choosing the destination: The question is whether we're heading toward heaven or hell.
  • Getting the ticket (Bible study): Find out what the journey asks of you. In the travel picture, count the cost, find a ride, and get your itinerary.
  • Checking in (going to church): Make connections and get familiar with the journey.
  • Going through TSA (commitment): Are you willing to let go of what you've been carrying?
  • Boarding (conversion): There comes a point when you have to make the decision to get on the plane.
2

The Flight: Christian Life

Takeoff: A new Christian

Scary
This life is unfamiliar. Can I endure it? Was I right to say yes?

Exciting
It's a new life with possibilities I couldn't see before.

En route: A growing Christian

A permanent mindset
I expect to get comfortable here. When I can't, I complain and stay agitated.

A temporary mindset
I can put up with some discomfort, stay grateful, and be patient. The flight isn't the destination.

Descent: An older Christian

  • The city is coming into view. Look ahead instead of looking back.
3

Landing: Dying Faithful

  • Picture the moment the wheels touch down. The turbulence is over. The journey is complete.
4

The Destination: Heaven

Greeted by God
The ultimate welcome home.

No baggage claim!
No sin, struggle, or sorrow left to carry.

The family of God
See the saints and be reunited with the faithful.

Conclusion

  • Where are you in the journey? Do you need to start studying the Bible? If you're already en route, are you complaining through the flight or learning to be grateful?
  • Keep your eyes on the destination. Heaven is worth the temporary discomfort of the flight.
