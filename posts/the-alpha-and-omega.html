Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Alpha and Omega

Seeing Jesus as the Alpha and Omega means recognizing that our relationship with Him is the only one that counts.

  • Alpha and Omega are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet. Think of it as “A to Z.”
  • In Revelation 1:8 the Lord God calls himself the Alpha and the Omega. In 1:17–18 the risen Jesus calls himself the First and the Last. Revelation 22:13 brings both titles together.

Big Idea

Seeing Jesus as the Alpha and Omega means recognizing that our relationship with Him is the only one that counts. When we stand before God, nothing else will matter.

1

The Relationship That Counts

"Not everyone who calls out to me, 'Lord! Lord!' will enter the Kingdom of Heaven. Only those who actually do the will of my Father in heaven will enter. On judgment day many will say to me, 'Lord! Lord!' ... But I will reply, 'I never knew you.'"

Matthew 7:21-23 (NLT)
  • We can be pulled by many relationships — family, friends, romance, marriage
  • But when you come to the end of your life, what relationship will God consider most important?
  • There's nothing more embarrassing than thinking you're in a relationship when the other person didn't get the memo
  • Think of the awkwardness of assuming you’re in a relationship only to discover that the other person understands it very differently.
  • Question: How is your relationship with Jesus?
    • Would He say you're in a relationship?
    • Do you spend quality time getting to know each other?
    • Do you read your Bible with the interest you would for someone you love?
  • If we don't take time now to know God, we'll face it when it's too late
2

A Window Into Judgment Day

"The Lord isn't really being slow about his promise, as some people think. No, he is being patient for your sake. He does not want anyone to be destroyed, but wants everyone to repent. But the day of the Lord will come as unexpectedly as a thief."

2 Peter 3:9-10 (NLT)
  • God's motive is not to send people to hell — He wants everyone to have a chance to change
  • Like a teacher who genuinely wants everyone to pass, not fail
  • But don't take this lightly — the Lord will come unexpectedly
  • Personal: My first encounter in Fresno — my car was stolen. The thief didn't announce his activity. It was there in the morning, gone that afternoon.
  • Being here today is an opportunity to find, develop, strengthen, or fight for your relationship with God
  • We don’t know how long we have. The thief illustration isn’t a calendar; it’s a call to be ready.
3

How Should We Live?

"Since everything around us is going to be destroyed like this, what holy and godly lives you should live, looking forward to the day of God... But we are looking forward to the new heavens and new earth he has promised."

2 Peter 3:11-13 (NLT)
  • Holy — Set apart, not the same as others, not following the crowd but the Bible
  • Godly — Aware of God's presence, thinking like Him, respecting Him, prioritizing Him
  • Looking forward to heaven — this is the good stuff
  • Judgment Day (Good scenario):
    • Waking up as your younger self, led to an incredible palace surrounded by clouds
    • God walks down from His throne to meet you, hugs you with what feels like a thousand arms
    • His warmth is indescribable, and He smells like homemade chocolate cookies
  • Judgment Day (Bad scenario):
    • Appearing before God as He searches a dusty book for your name
    • No smile, just heartache — like He doesn't know you
    • "Away from me, I never knew you"
    • Nothing else matters — just utter regret for living selfishly

Conclusion

  • Summary: At that moment before God, nothing else will matter — school, degree, job, finding "the one," financial stability, family — just your relationship with Him
  • Call to Action: Make every effort today to figure out if you are in the relationship God desires. Ask to study the Bible and find out more about God's love for you.
  • Closing: He is the Alpha and the Omega — the beginning and the end, and everything in between.
