Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

Praying In The Spirit

Praying in the Spirit means being led and prompted by the Holy Spirit toward God; leaving room for the Spirit to inspire and urge you in prayer.

  • Theme: Prayer — needed to accomplish God's great task of seeing all people saved
  • Prayer can be the most misunderstood, misused, underutilized, and mysterious part of Christian walk
  • Prayer is one of our strongest tools, yet it’s easy to neglect.

Big Idea

Praying in the Spirit means being led and prompted by the Holy Spirit toward God — leaving room for the Spirit to inspire and urge you in prayer.

1

What Is Praying "In The Spirit"?

"Pray in the Spirit at all times and on every occasion. Stay alert and be persistent in your prayers for all believers everywhere."

Ephesians 6:18 (NLT)
  • First: To pray in the Spirit, you need the Spirit (Acts 2:38 — repent, be baptized, receive the gift)
  • Translation from NLV: "You must pray at all times as the Holy Spirit leads you to pray"
  • Commentary definition: "As the Spirit prompts (or urges) you to pray" or "as the Spirit suggests that you should pray"
  • Galatians 4:6: "God has sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, prompting us to call out, 'Abba, Father'"
  • The Spirit is in our hearts to prompt us toward God!
  • Question: Do you leave room for the Spirit to speak? Is your prayer life rushed, distracted, or non-existent?
2

Personal Story of Spirit-Led Prayer

"And because we are his children, God has sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, prompting us to call out, 'Abba, Father.'"

Galatians 4:6 (NLT)
  • Personal: After a difficult conversation, I left unsettled and reached for one podcast after another, anything but silence.
  • Felt uneasy, heart weighed down — a voice kept prompting: "You should be praying"
  • Resistant at first, but couldn't ignore it — started praying
  • Now I often use that drive to pray, and I read Scripture when I can sit down with it later.
  • When tempted to listen to podcasts, the Spirit reminds me whose time it is!
  • Questions: Are you ignoring the Spirit prompting you? When was the last time you stopped and prayed because the Spirit urged you?
3

How Do We Pray "In The Spirit"?

"Are any of you suffering hardships? You should pray. Are any of you happy? You should sing praises... The earnest prayer of a righteous person has great power and produces wonderful results."

James 5:13, 16 (NLT)
  • Paul's guidelines (Ephesians 6:18):
  • (1) All times and Every Occasion (James 5:13-17)
    • If praying all times in many ways, the Spirit has more opportunities to move
    • Hard for Spirit to lead when you're not available
    • Challenge: Think of times and ways you can pray differently for each topic
  • (2) Stay Alert (Colossians 4:2)
    • Eliminate distractions, give moments for spontaneous prayer
    • The Spirit can move any minute — God is not on OUR schedule
    • Challenge: Set reminders throughout the day (like 10:02). Be alert to prayer requests.
  • (3) Be Persistent (Luke 18:1-8)
    • Even though the Spirit leads, responsibility is still on us to continue praying
    • Can't pray one incredible prayer and think we've done our duty
    • Challenge: Stick to your decisions about prayer life. Set a goal for consistency.

Conclusion

  • Summary: Praying in the Spirit = being led by the Spirit. Do it all the time, stay alert, be persistent.
  • Call to Action: Commit to this throughout the summer. Lay the foundation for Spirit-led prayer.
  • Closing: God is not on your prayer schedule — schedule your prayer around God!
