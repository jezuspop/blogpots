Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Bible Inspired

The Bible is the greatest book ever written; it works in every culture, background, and situation.

  • Year theme: Devoted — this month: Devoted to Understanding
  • Deeper understanding of God's word leads to inspiration, which leads to hope
  • Being inspired and hopeful in God can get us through anything — any obstacle, hardship, or discouragement

Big Idea

The Bible is the greatest book ever written — it works in every culture, background, and situation. If God were to write a book, this is what it would look like.

1

The Bible's Declining Influence

"But in your hearts revere Christ as Lord. Always be prepared to give an answer to everyone who asks you to give the reason for the hope that you have."

1 Peter 3:15 (NIV)
  • The role of the Bible in my life prior: THE BIG WHITE BOOK (sacred but untouched)
  • A Bible can be respected as sacred and still sit unopened. I know what that looks like because I lived it.
  • Challenge: Read the Bible all the way through. I made it my conviction to read through it every year.
2

The Bible's Fascination

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... And the Word became flesh, and dwelt among us, and we saw His glory..."

John 1:1, 14 (NASB)
  • What if there was a book that guarantees: happy life, success, financial peace, best friends, great student, lasting marriage, great parenting, total forgiveness, healing the soul?
  • John's opening: Before the world began, the Word existed — with God and was God
  • God sent Jesus as His Word — this book is a special, personal message from our Father
  • To be inspired is to know God. To know God is to know Jesus. To know Jesus is to know His Word.
3

Divine Revelation

"Above all, you must understand that no prophecy of Scripture came about by the prophet's own interpretation of things. For prophecy never had its origin in the human will, but prophets, though human, spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit."

2 Peter 1:20-21 (NIV)
  • No prophecy comes from human opinion — rather from God Himself through the Holy Spirit
  • The Bible brings together many writers, genres, and centuries around one thread I keep coming back to: God’s relationship with His people.
4

The Greatest Proof — It Works!

Jesus compares someone who hears and practices His teaching to a builder who digs deep and lays the foundation on rock. When the flood comes, the house stands. (Paraphrase.)

Luke 6:47–48
  • Jesus boldly preaches His words are the only foundation to sustain life
  • You will not be shaken by any circumstance life throws your way!
  • It works — you can try it in your personal, family, social, academic, job, dating, marriage, parenting life
  • It transcends time and languages, breaks down barriers
  • No other book speaks to atheist and theist, republican and democrat, dictator and pacifist
  • IF GOD WERE TO WRITE A BOOK, THIS IS WHAT IT WOULD LOOK LIKE!

Conclusion

  • Summary: Take seriously the divine power you hold in your hands — the word of truth, the word of God
  • Call to Action: The more we give it rightful place in our lives, the more inspired we will be. Read your Bible like never before.
  • Closing: LIVE THE BIBLE INSPIRED.
