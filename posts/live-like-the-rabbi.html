Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

Live Like The Rabbi

Jesus chose us to follow Him and make disciples; the most powerful being to ever walk the earth has called you to follow Him, not just to know the…

  • Focus: Deeply Rooted in Fellowship
  • Looking at the example of Jesus in the fellowship—how He gave and His calling for the fellowship
  • Jesus' calling for the fellowship was to be Disciple Makers

Big Idea

Jesus chose us to follow Him and make disciples—the most powerful being to ever walk the earth has called you to follow Him, not just to know the material but to deeply know the person.

1

Learning as a Disciple

"Then Jesus came to them and said, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.'"

Matthew 28:18-20 (NIV)

A talmid is a disciple or student. Think about the difference between taking notes in a classroom and learning to live from someone you follow. Expectation of the Talmid:

  • Total devotion (heart, mind, soul, strength)
  • Not just knowing the material but deeply knowing the person
  • In Pirkei Avot 1:4, Yose ben Yoezer uses the image of the dust of wise teachers’ feet.
  • The idea: follow so closely behind your rabbi that the dust of his feet would cover you
2

Jesus Was Different—He Chose Us

"You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you might go and bear fruit—fruit that will last—and so that whatever you ask in my name the Father will give you."

John 15:16 (NIV)
  • In John 15, Jesus tells the disciples that He chose them. The Gospels show Him calling working fishermen and a tax collector (Matthew 4:18–22; 9:9).
  • To me, that initiative is a picture of humility and love.
  • Imagine what these men must have felt to hear these words!
3

We Are Christ's Ambassadors

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here! All this is from God, who reconciled us to himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation... We are therefore Christ's ambassadors, as though God were making his appeal through us."

2 Corinthians 5:17-20 (NIV)
  • We all were chosen to follow Jesus
  • We were given the ministry of reconciliation
  • What does that practically mean? Bringing reconciliation to family, friends, all relationships
  • What Jesus has called us to is infinitely profound, important, and mind-blowing!

Conclusion

  • Summary: Jesus' expectation for His Talmidim then is the same for us now—make disciples
  • Call to Action: Be ambassadors for Christ; meet people, study the Bible with them, follow up
  • Closing: Commit to have the same expectation in our lives that Jesus did in His—leading others to become His Talmidim
