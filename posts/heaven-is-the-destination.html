Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

Heaven is The Destination

Heaven is the destination. Everything from conversion to death prepares us for the arrival.

  • The Christian life is like airplane travel. Think from pre-flight to arrival: each stage gives us a picture of a different part of the journey.
  • What happens when we start expecting the flight itself to feel like the destination?

Big Idea

Heaven is the destination. Everything from conversion to death prepares us for the arrival.

1

Pre-Flight: Before Conversion

"Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life, and only a few find it."

Matthew 7:13-14 (NIV)

Choose where you're headed

  • Jesus describes two roads, one leading to life and the other to destruction. Where are you headed?
  • Getting the ticket (Bible study): Count the cost, find a ride, get the itinerary. The ticket is part of the picture, not a claim that we buy our way into heaven.
  • Checking in (going to church): Meet the people you'll be traveling with.
  • TSA (commitment): What are you willing to leave behind?
  • Boarding (conversion): Make the decision and step onto the plane.
2

Takeoff: A New Christian

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"

2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV)

The new life feels new

Scary
You're not used to it. Can you endure? You might second-guess the decision.

Exciting
You're in Christ. A whole new life has begun.

3

En Route: A Growing Christian

"Not that I have already obtained all this, or have already arrived at my goal, but I press on to take hold of that for which Christ Jesus took hold of me."

Philippians 3:12 (NIV)

Don't get too comfortable on the flight

  • Permanent mindset: I need to get comfortable. When that doesn't happen, I complain and stay agitated.
  • Temporary mindset: This flight won't last forever. I can sit with some discomfort, stay grateful, and learn patience.
  • Paul's still pressing on. So keep going, even when the flight gets rough.
4

Descent: An Older Christian

"I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith. Now there is in store for me the crown of righteousness..."

2 Timothy 4:7–8 (NIV excerpt)

Look ahead

  • Paul looks back on a race finished and faith kept, and ahead to the crown.
  • The city is coming into view. Look forward, not behind. Get ready for landing.
5

Landing: Dying Faithful

"Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on... they will rest from their labor, for their deeds will follow them."

Revelation 14:13 (NIV excerpt)

Touching down in eternity

  • Revelation calls those who die in the Lord blessed. Their labor gives way to rest, and their deeds follow them.
  • Stay faithful to the end.
6

The Destination: Heaven

"'He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death' or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away."

Revelation 21:4 (NIV)

No baggage claim!

Greeted by God
The ultimate welcome. The vision is of God dwelling with his people (Revelation 21:3).

No baggage claim
No more death, mourning, crying, or pain.

The family of God
Picture the joy of seeing the saints and being reunited with the faithful.

Conclusion

  • Where are you in the journey: pre-flight, just taking off, en route, or approaching landing?
  • Heaven is the destination. Stay on the flight. The arrival will be worth it.
