Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Christmas Story

Christmas means GOD with us (He deserves our worship) and God WITH US (we can approach Him with trust and intimacy).

  • This time of year, countless movies try to capture the meaning of Christmas
  • AMC Top 10: It's A Wonderful Life, National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation, Rudolph, Home Alone, A Charlie Brown Christmas...
  • But what is THE Christmas Story?

Big Idea

Christmas means GOD with us (He deserves our worship) and God WITH US (we can approach Him with trust and intimacy). Don't let the holidays blind you from the true meaning.

1

God With Us — His Divinity

"All this took place to fulfill what the Lord had said through the prophet: 'The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel' (which means 'God with us')."

Matthew 1:22-23 (NIV)
  • Matthew introduces Jesus to the Jews by linking Him with King David and Abraham
  • Jesus is Immanuel — GOD with us — foretold by Isaiah
  • Jewish disciples worshiped the God of Israel. What stands out in Matthew’s account is that those same disciples worship the risen Jesus.
    • Angels refuse worship (Revelation 22:8-9), but Jesus didn't (Matthew 28:9-10, 16-17)
    • Jesus forgave sins — only God can do that!
  • Why did these Jews think Jesus was God? Because they had been WITH God (Acts 4:13)
  • When someone is deified, it usually doesn't come from family/close friends — but these disciples LIVED with Him!
  • Questions: How do you see Jesus? Have you lost the awe the Apostles had? Does His divinity give you faith He can save you?
2

God With Us — His Intimacy

"The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us. We have seen his glory, the glory of the one and only Son, who came from the Father, full of grace and truth."

John 1:14 (NIV)
  • God, Creator, Master, Holy Being put Himself in the form of "withness"
  • Every time prior to Jesus when God's presence came near, it was terrifying: Job's whirlwind, Abraham's smoking furnace, Moses' blinding greatness, pillar of fire
  • Notice John's language describing God in Jesus:
    • Light of Men (illumination)
    • Became Flesh (tangible)
    • Dwelt among us (took up residence)
    • Full of Grace and Truth (approachable)
    • Made Him known (to explain, relate)
  • The Apostles weren't merely in Jesus' presence — they were WITH Him
  • Illustration: Being in the presence of an NFL team isn't the same as being WITH them — staying at their home, watching their kids, being in weddings, attending funerals
  • Questions: Do you feel you are WITH Jesus? Do you know His word? Are you plugged into His church and fellowship?

Conclusion

  • Summary: The Christmas Story = GOD with us (He deserves more worship than we give) and God WITH US (we can approach Him with trust and intimacy)
  • Call to Action: Don't let the holidays blind you from the true meaning of Christmas
  • Closing: If He is "God with us," He deserves our worship. If He is "God WITH us," we can approach Him in our time of need.
