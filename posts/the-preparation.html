Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Preparation

From the beginning of John’s Gospel to Jesus’ promise of a place with the Father, God’s preparation shows us how much he wants us with him.

  • Theme: Jesus—leading up to the birth of Jesus
  • God's preparation in the birth of Jesus translates His incredible love for us
  • I remember how much planning went into getting ready to welcome our first child. Now picture a plan that reaches back before creation.

Big Idea

God's preparation for sending His Son was a plan from eternity—Jesus was always the way, the truth, and the life, and God is still preparing a place for us.

Background: John's Gospel

  • John opens differently from Matthew, Mark, and Luke. It begins with the Word already with God before creation. That opening tells us where to look for God’s plan: before Jesus’ earthly life even began.
1

Preparing for The Son

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. He was with God in the beginning. Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made. In him was life, and that life was the light of all mankind."

John 1:1-4 (NIV)
  • God's preparation was a plan long before we existed—it wasn't a backup plan!
  • Jesus was there always from the beginning
  • The Greek word "was" has continuous action—like a motion picture, not a snapshot
  • "Jesus Christ the same yesterday and today and forever" (Hebrews 13:8)
  • Jesus is at the center of the universe—everything we see was made through Him

The Word "Life" (Zo-eh):

  • Greeks had several words for life: Bios (physical), Psuche (breath/animating), Zo-eh (the WAY to live)
  • John uses "zo-eh"—Jesus is the force that gives life!
  • "I am the way, and the truth, and the LIFE; no one comes to the Father but through Me" (John 14:6)

If you're not a Christian, you're missing out on living life the way it was meant to be lived:

  • Like buying a sports car and only driving in first gear
  • Like buying a 4K television and watching static fuzz
  • The life in Jesus is truly HIGH DEFINITION!
2

Preparing for His People

"Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God—children born not of natural descent, nor of human decision or a husband's will, but born of God."

John 1:12-13 (NIV)
  • In God's preparation sending Jesus to be born, He was also preparing for OUR spiritual birth
  • If you're visiting, I challenge you to study the Bible
  • Find out what it means to be born of God—to really believe in His name, not just saying a prayer
3

Preparing a Place for Us

"Don't let your hearts be troubled. Trust in God, and trust also in me. There is more than enough room in my Father's home. If this were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? When everything is ready, I will come and get you, so that you will always be with me where I am."

John 14:1-3 (NLT)
  • Like a parent preparing for their soon-to-be child:
    • Decorating the room
    • Picking out colors and themes
    • Arranging furniture and toys
    • Waiting for the arrival of new life
  • He has done the same for you and me!

Conclusion

  • Summary: God's preparation and plan translates to His incredible love—from eternity past, to our spiritual birth, to our eternal home
  • Call to Action: Study the Bible if you're not a Christian; appreciate the depth of God's preparation
  • Closing: Let us not forget that God's Preparation and Plan translates to His incredible love
