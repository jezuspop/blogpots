Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Big Three

The Big Three: Love God with crazy devotion. Love each other as Jesus loved us. Love the lost enough to change our approach for their sake.

  • Sharing our core convictions we hope to impress onto you
  • Start where Jesus starts when he’s asked which commandment matters most.

Big Idea

The Big Three: Love God with crazy devotion. Love each other as Jesus loved us. Love the lost enough to change our approach for their sake.

Foundation: The Most Important Commandment

Jesus answers a question about the greatest commandment

One of the teachers of religious law was standing there listening to the debate. He realized that Jesus had answered well, so he asked, 'Of all the commandments, which is the most important?' Jesus replied, 'The most important commandment is this: "Listen, O Israel! The LORD our God is the one and only LORD. And you must love the LORD your God with all your heart, all your soul, all your mind, and all your strength." The second is equally important: "Love your neighbor as yourself." No other commandment is greater than these.'

Mark 12:28–31 (NLT)

Cultural Background: The Shema

  • Jesus begins with the Shema, Israel’s confession that opens with “Hear, O Israel.” Deuteronomy tells families to teach these words through the ordinary rhythms of the day. Jesus pairs them with Leviticus’s call to love one’s neighbor, given alongside commands against vengeance and grudges. Deuteronomy 6:4–9 · Leviticus 19:15–18 · Mark 12:28–31
1

Love God (Crazy Love)

Paul on the value of knowing Christ

I once thought these things were valuable, but now I consider them worthless because of what Christ has done. Yes, everything else is worthless when compared with the infinite value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord.

Philippians 3:7–8 (NLT, excerpt)
  • How does Paul view his faith from this passage?
  • Your walk with God is the SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT thing in your life (Paul's conviction in verse 7)
  • When I get with people who are struggling in faith, sin, or discouragement, my first question is: How is your walk with God?
  • Paul’s attitude: Don’t live in the past. Look ahead and keep pressing toward the goal (Philippians 3:13–14, paraphrase).
2

Love Each Other

Jesus tells his disciples how to love

My command is this: Love each other as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than this: to lay down his life for his friends. You are my friends if you do what I command. I no longer call you servants, because a servant does not know his master's business. Instead, I have called you friends, for everything that I learned from my Father I have made known to you.

John 15:12–15 (NIV)
  • Jesus says love each other AS HE HAS LOVED US
  • How did Jesus show his love for the disciples?
  • How do you feel when someone's love is not genuine? When you sense they're doing it out of obligation?
  • If our love is not like Jesus toward each other, we will not be quick and willing to lay down our lives for one another
3

Love the Lost

1 Corinthians 9:19–23
  • How does Paul show his love for the lost through this passage?
  • How far are you willing to go to win someone to Christ?
  • Love the lost enough to change what can change: our habits, comfort, or approach. Paul adapts for the sake of the gospel without stepping outside Christ’s law (1 Corinthians 9:21–23).

Conclusion

  • Summary: The Big Three core convictions—Love God with crazy devotion, Love each other as Jesus loved, Love the lost enough to become all things
  • Call to Action: Evaluate your life against these three priorities
  • Closing: Jesus calls love for God and neighbor the greatest commandments. The Big Three are how we want to live them.
