The Ascension

What did Jesus give his followers when he returned to the Father, and why does that matter to us now?

  • During times of instability, my faith gets tested: Can I focus on Jesus? Can I find security in my relationship with God?
  • I go back to the Ascension — it puts things in perspective
  • Questions like: "I'd be better off if Jesus walked with me like the Apostles" or "Who is qualified to teach me?"
  • Wouldn't it be awesome to sit at Jesus' feet, take Him to coffee, get His advice?

Big Idea

Through the Ascension, Jesus offers something greater than physical presence — He becomes infinitely available to everyone through the Holy Spirit. The least in the kingdom is greater than John the Baptist.

1

The Importance of the Ascension

"Jesus said, 'Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet ascended to the Father. Go instead to my brothers and tell them, I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.'"

John 20:17 (NIV)
  • Mary Magdalene was close to Jesus — cured of 7 demons, at the crucifixion with His family
  • She was trying to hold on for dear life — didn't want to lose Him again
  • Strange response: "Do not hold on to me, for I have not yet ascended"
  • What Jesus was saying: Mary was thinking "I don't want to lose you again." Jesus was thinking "I'm making it impossible for that to ever happen."
  • Jesus was offering something more fulfilling than physical connection
2

Jesus Alluded to This Earlier

"And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another advocate to help you and be with you forever... I will not leave you as orphans; I will come to you... On that day you will realize that I am in my Father, and you are in me, and I am in you."

John 14:16-18, 20 (NIV)
  • (1) Their relationship will get better because of the Ascension — "You should be glad I am going to the Father" (v28)
  • (2) If Jesus didn't ascend, the Holy Spirit couldn't be with us
  • Jesus in physical form was limited — one place at one time
  • Imagine trying to see Him today:
    • Waiting list for prayer time, counseling, weddings, Bible studies
    • People buying tickets, bringing sick family, hecklers, dangers
  • Through the Ascension: Jesus expanded across the universe, giving each baptized disciple the Holy Spirit
  • Infinitely available: No lines, no stress, full access
  • Colossians 1:27: "This is the secret: Christ lives in you"
3

The Power of the Ascension

"I tell you, among those born of women there is no one greater than John; yet the one who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he."

Luke 7:28 (NIV)
  • Jesus calls John the Baptist the greatest prophet — greater than Abraham, Moses, Elijah, Isaiah, Daniel!
  • But the ending: "The least in the kingdom is GREATER than John!"
  • Somewhere in the world is the weakest Christian — most sinful, insecure, faithless, troubled
  • That person is greater than John the Baptist and all the prophets!
  • Why? Because of the Ascension
  • The Holy Spirit is in every one of us — our own personal Jesus to take everywhere
  • 1 Corinthians 6:19: "Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit"

Conclusion

  • Summary: You have a greater chance at a close relationship with Jesus than those who physically walked with Him. We are the hands and feet of Jesus.
  • Call to Action: No excuse to not grow deeply. No excuse to say you can't change. The Apostles didn't have the best God could offer — you do!
  • Closing: View your faith and connection to Jesus as real as if He were with you in physical form. The Ascension makes it true.
