Topical Sermons November 24, 2026

The Rabbi Jesus

Jesus called working fishermen and a tax collector to follow Him. The One who chose ordinary people still pursues us even when we fail.

  • Looking at Jesus' relationships—how He loved, taught, and related
  • Focusing on His relationship with the Apostles prior to His ministry
  • See the incredible heart Jesus had for these men

Big Idea

Jesus called working fishermen and a tax collector to follow Him. The One who chose ordinary people still pursues us even when we fail.

1

Learning as a Disciple

A talmid is a disciple or student. Learning from a teacher meant more than knowing the material; that’s the picture I want us to keep. Expectation of the Talmid:

  • Total devotion—spending entire time listening and observing the teacher
  • Not just knowing material but deeply knowing the PERSON
  • In Pirkei Avot 1:4, Yose ben Yoezer uses the image of the dust of wise teachers’ feet.
  • Follow so closely behind your rabbi that his dust covers you—his teaching should be visibly covering you
2

Jesus Was Different: He Chose Us

"You didn't choose me. I chose you. I appointed you to go and produce lasting fruit, so that the Father will give you whatever you ask for, using my name... I chose you to come out of the world."

John 15:16, 19 (NLT)
  • The Gospels show Jesus calling working fishermen and a tax collector (Matthew 4:18–22; 9:9). In John 15, He reminds the disciples of His initiative.
  • Imagine being called by Jesus in the middle of your ordinary working day!

"Remember, dear brothers and sisters, that few of you were wise in the world's eyes or powerful or wealthy when God called you. Instead, God chose things the world considers foolish in order to shame those who think they are wise."

1 Corinthians 1:26-27 (NLT)
  • We all were chosen to follow Jesus
  • We don’t need a résumé that impresses the world in order to follow Jesus.
  • What Jesus has called us to is infinitely profound, important, and mind-blowing!

Illustration: If George Lucas sought me out, sent his helicopter to take me to Skywalker Ranch, and told me he wanted to mentor me to run Lucasfilm... I would lose my mind! But what Jesus calls us to is infinitely greater!

3

To Be Worthy Talmidim

"I once thought these things were valuable, but now I consider them worthless because of what Christ has done. Yes, everything else is worthless when compared with the infinite value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord... I want to know Christ and experience the mighty power that raised him from the dead."

Philippians 3:7-8, 10 (NLT)

Question: How well do you know the Master?

  • The expectation is the same as for the ancient Talmid:
    • Total devotion to our Rabbi
    • Spend our entire lives listening and observing Jesus
    • Know Scripture and put it into practice just as Jesus did
4

He Is Still Calling You

"Jesus said, 'Simon son of John, do you love me?'... 'Feed my sheep... Follow me!'"

John 21:15-19 (NIV)
  • This is the difference with Rabbi Jesus—even when we fail, He still pursues us because we are chosen
  • Tim Cook won't come pursuing you if you got fired from the Apple Store—but Jesus will!
  • The CEO and Founder of Humanity will pursue you personally
  • Jesus hasn't given up on any of us
5

The Alternative Warning

"Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter the kingdom of heaven... Then I will tell them plainly, 'I never knew you.'"

Matthew 7:21, 23 (NLT)
  • These followers had failed to do the most important task of a Talmid: following their Rabbi
  • The scathing remark: "I never KNEW you"—they failed to truly know Him
  • Let's not find our Christianity in a place like this

Conclusion

  • Summary: Jesus calls ordinary people and pursues us even when we fail. What kind of disciple will we become?
  • Call to Action: What will you do this week to change how you've been following Jesus?
  • Closing: How much dust from the Gospels is covering your Christian faith?
